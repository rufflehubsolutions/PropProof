Sattva City North Bengaluru: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official RERA registration number for Sattva City?
Sattva City is registered under Karnataka RERA with registration number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/472/PR/270226/008494, verified directly from official state portal disclosures.

What is the expected completion date for Sattva City?
The statutory completion timeline registered with RERA indicates a February 28, 2032 completion deadline across its multi-parcel configuration.

Where is Sattva City located and what configurations are offered?
The project spans roughly 49+ acres across Doddajala and Meenukunte villages, Jala Hobli, Yelahanka Taluk, Bengaluru North along NH-44. It features 31 towers comprising 3,298 units with 2 BHK, 3 BHK, and 4 BHK configurations.

What financial disclosures are tracked in our report?
