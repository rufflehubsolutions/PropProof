How to Verify RERA Project Compliance Before Paying Your Booking Token

Published by PropProof Research Team • 6 min read

Before handing over an advance for an under-construction property, verifying registration numbers is vital. Through our independent real estate intelligence platform, we track statutory disclosures to help buyers cut through marketing noise. However, glancing at a certificate is never enough; inspecting live quarterly portal disclosures is required to gauge real-world developer health.

Use this framework to evaluate compliance before financial commitment:

1. Check the Financial Escrow Status

Regulatory frameworks require customer collections to route through designated escrow accounts. Reviewing certified financial filings confirms whether funds remain locked exclusively for construction progress. For example, examining live filings for developments like Godrej Aveline gives a clear window into how statutory collection velocities match project milestones.

2. Compare Capital Collected vs. Physical Construction

Cross-examine reported customer advances against certified physical tower milestones. Discrepancies where capital inflows outpace site progress signal potential cash-flow vulnerabilities.

3. Review Statutory Completion Timelines

Verify registered deadlines filed with the state authority to ensure project milestones align with your personal financial planning and mortgage requirements.

4. Scan for Public Dispute Records

Examine portal litigation sections to confirm the land parcel is free from active injunctions or third-party ownership challenges.

Key Takeaway:

Government portal filings provide binding commitments that override promotional marketing materials.
